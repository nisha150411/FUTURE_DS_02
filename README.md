Customer Churn Analysis

This dashboard analyzes customer behavior and identifies the key reasons behind customer churn. The main goal is to help businesses improve customer retention and reduce customer loss through data-driven insights.

1. Why are customers leaving the platform?
The dashboard shows that customer churn is mainly influenced by:
Higher monthly charges
Short customer tenure (new customers churn more)
Certain contract types (month-to-month customers have higher churn)
Lack of additional services like tech support and online security

2. Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
The most likely customers to churn are:
Month-to-month contract customers
Customers with lower tenure
Senior citizens Senior citizens (in many churn datasets this segment shows higher churn)
Customers using electronic check payment method

3. How long do customers typically stay active?
Using tenure analysis:
Most churned customers leave within the first 1–2 years.
Long-term customers are more likely to stay loyal.

4. What actions can improve customer retention?
Suggested actions based on dashboard insights:
Offer discounts or loyalty rewards for new customers
Encourage long-term contracts
Improve customer support services
Promote value-added services like tech support and online security
